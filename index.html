About us

A pioneer in local dentistry

Since 2007, Degla Dental Clinic has grown into one of the leading dental providers in Maadi, Cairo, thanks to multiple specialties and consistently high quality of care.

We are proud to treat celebrities, embassies and multinational companies seeking excellent dental care for their staff.

Visit the clinic
Dr. Maher Haroun
Owner & Director · Degla Dental Clinic

Dr. Maher Haroun is the owner and director of the clinic. Educated and trained in Egypt and Germany, he has more than 30 years of experience across dental specialties. He is a certified implant specialist and expert from Cairo University, with specialized German training in dental implantology.

Dr. Mina Maher Haroun
Owner · Degla Dental Clinic

Dr. Mina Maher is an owner of Degla Dental Clinic. He graduated from Misr University for Science and Technology and trained at Cairo University Hospital and the Air Force, with further implant training from the American implant dentistry association and New York University. He holds ADA CERP accreditation in root canal treatment and in implant dentistry, plus Cairo University certificates in both fields. He is a member of the German Board of Oral Implantology (GBOI) and the German Association of Dental Implantology (DGZI), and holds a fellowship in laser dentistry from the University of Genoa.
